P01 / Psychosocial Integrity

Notice the Therapeutic Cue

  • Patient says they are frightened before a procedure.
  • Recognize the emotion before moving to teaching.
  • Ask focused, open-ended questions.
When a patient says they are frightened before a procedure, the first nursing move is to notice that emotional cue. Therapeutic communication starts by acknowledging the feeling, then assessing what the patient understands and what concern is driving the fear.

P02 / Psychosocial Integrity

Choose the Best First Response

  • Avoid false reassurance.
  • Avoid minimizing the concern.
  • Reflect emotion and clarify meaning.
A common testing trap is choosing advice or reassurance too soon. Responses like do not worry or everything will be fine can minimize the patient's concern. A stronger response reflects emotion and asks the patient to say more.

P03 / Psychosocial Integrity

Clinical Judgment Check

  • Recognize cues: fear statement.
  • Analyze cues: meaning and understanding.
  • Take action: acknowledge, assess, invite.
This item aligns to clinical judgment because the nurse must recognize the cue, analyze what it means, take a therapeutic action, and evaluate whether the response helped the patient communicate their concern.
