Therapeutic communication in nursing requires the nurse to notice patient cues, ask focused open-ended questions, reflect emotion, and avoid false reassurance. When a patient says they are frightened before a procedure, the nurse should acknowledge the feeling, assess understanding, and invite the patient to describe the concern. This supports the clinical judgment steps of recognizing cues, analyzing cues, and taking action. A common testing trap is choosing a response that gives advice or minimizes the concern instead of assessing the patient's meaning.

NCLEX alignment: Psychosocial Integrity; Clinical Judgment Measurement Model: Recognize Cues, Analyze Cues, Take Action, Evaluate Outcomes; Nursing Process: Assessment and Implementation; Bloom level: Apply.
