## Final readiness checklist — ship the 1‑Hour Blueprint and start selling

One door. One fast win. One upsell. Make every step increase outcome/likelihood and cut time/effort—the value equation guides scope [$100M Offers, Page 59]. Keep time‑to‑value under 5 minutes—speed sells [$100M Offers, Page 65].

### 1) Promise, names, and guarantee (lock these)
- Free: “1‑Hour Exam Recovery Blueprint” (on‑page + PDF + email).
- Paid: “Complete Study Blueprint + Sprint Pack” ($19–39).
- Guarantee on checkout: “If it doesn’t reveal 3 high‑yield fixes you didn’t know, 7‑day refund.” Guarantees lift perceived likelihood and conversion [$100M Offers, Page 135; $100M Offers, Page 65].

### 2) Product delivery (fast, multi‑format)
- Deliver the free result instantly on‑page and via PDF/email. Packaging in multiple formats increases consumption and leads [$100M Leads, Page 43].
- SLA: free <5 minutes; paid same‑day. Shorter perceived delay = more value [$100M Offers, Page 65].

### 3) Upsell contrast (make the paid feel obvious)
- On the free result page, show locked vs unlocked: 
  - Free: top 1–2 gaps + 3×20‑min tasks + 5 links.
  - Paid: full ranked list + 3–5 Sprints + 20–30 links + checklist.
- Make the contrast huge—premium wins when the decoy is basic [$100M Money Models, Page 49].

### 4) Creative engine (hooks drive sales)
- Write 50 post‑exam hooks; test 3–5 “meats” (demo/testimonial/explainer). Spend 80% of prep on hooks—ads are made in prep, not recording [$100M Playbook: GOATed Ads, Page 9; $100M Playbook: GOATed Ads, Page 29].
- Match awareness (problem/solution/product) to widen reach [$100M Playbook: GOATed Ads, Page 11].

### 5) Proof loop (raise believability)
- Screenshot Real Blueprints, before/after score deltas, and student quotes. Proof directly increases perceived likelihood of success [$100M Offers, Page 65].

### 6) Analytics + KPIs (decide with data)
- Track: landing_view, upload_complete, mini_view, checkout_open, purchase, full_sent.
- Benchmarks (first 2–4 weeks): 
  - Opt‑in ≥30%, Intake ≥50%, Upsell 5–10%, Free result <5 min.
- If weak: fix hooks first, then tighten the free output clarity—don’t add features [$100M Leads, Page 46].

### 7) Pricing and packaging (simple tests)
- A/B the paid at $19 vs $29 vs $39 (even split) for 1–2 weeks. Keep the deliverable identical.

### 8) Sequence next (after signal)
- Once the upsell converts, add “Plan Refill” continuity (weekly Sprint delivery). Build stages in order—simple scales, fancy fails [$100M Money Models, Page 155].

> I built everything by doing more reps. Ship this simple funnel, buy data, and iterate. Winners win because they decide to.

### Call to action
Rename outputs, add the guarantee, ship the free result in multi‑format under 5 minutes, bolt on the high‑contrast upsell, and launch 50 hooks this week. Then judge by the KPIs—fix hooks and clarity before adding features.
